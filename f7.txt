1. DESIGN DIRECTION
==================================================

VISUAL STYLE:
Tropical Brutalism / Afro-Tropical Festival / Editorial Brutalism.

The main visual reference is the "Tropical Brutalism" concept:
- intense yellow
- deep black
- dark purple
- electric orange accents
- tropical photography
- oversized typography
- hard rectangular containers
- thick borders
- asymmetrical editorial composition
- high contrast
- aggressive visual hierarchy
- raw poster/flyer aesthetic
- contemporary festival website
- premium modern web design
- responsive and conversion-focused

The design should feel like:
"an award-winning 2026 festival website mixed with a contemporary Afro-Caribbean poster."

Avoid generic SaaS aesthetics.
Avoid rounded corporate cards.
Avoid excessive glassmorphism.
Avoid soft gradients as the dominant visual language.
Avoid generic Bootstrap-looking components.

The design should feel bold, cultural, youthful, energetic and premium.
